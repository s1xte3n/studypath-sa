# ADR-002 — Curated Catalogue, Provenance and Deterministic Eligibility

- **Status:** Proposed design consistent with approved requirements; owner acceptance of this ADR pending.
- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Requirements:** REQ-CAT-001, REQ-MAT-001, REQ-ALT-001, REQ-DET-001, REQ-FUND-001, NFR-DATA-001.
- **Related:** OR-005, OR-007, OR-008, OR-013, Q-002, Q-019.

## Context
Admissions and funding requirements vary by institution and cycle. Unsupported rules can produce harmful false-positive eligibility claims. The approved pilot is bounded and curated, not a claim of national coverage.

## Decision
Propose a versioned catalogue with institution/programme/subject/rule/funding/source/version/relationship concepts. Every rule, deadline and eligibility statement must trace to authoritative source evidence, cycle, verification date, catalogue version and curator sign-off. Publish only complete supported rules. Eligibility is deterministic and returns `ELIGIBLE`, `NOT_ELIGIBLE`, or `UNKNOWN`; incomplete, stale, ambiguous or unrepresentable rules cannot yield `ELIGIBLE`. Alternatives require a verified explicit relationship. Use a synthetic golden set of at least 20 cases and target zero false-positive ELIGIBLE results before release.

## Alternatives considered
1. AI-generated eligibility: rejected as authoritative decision logic.
2. Hard-coded rules scattered in UI: rejected because they are difficult to audit and update safely.
3. Automated national scraping: deferred; adds provenance, source-use, quality and operations risk beyond the bounded pilot.
4. Treat missing requirements as satisfied: rejected; must produce UNKNOWN or withhold the record.

## Trade-offs and consequences
- Curation takes effort and limits catalogue breadth, but improves explainability and safety.
- Rule composition and points-system differences require explicit schema and tests; a generic AND/OR assumption is unsafe.
- Source freshness needs recurring review and release controls.
- No database is required for the local demo; storage technology remains an infrastructure decision.

## Open implementation decisions
Freeze canonical subject IDs, supported operators/composition, APS/points support, cycle semantics, exact pilot records, relationship evidence and publication workflow before implementing the evaluator/API. No golden-set execution or publication-ready record count is claimed.
