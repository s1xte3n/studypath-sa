## Safety and interpretation rules

- These are **NSC minimum-requirement drafts**, not complete admissions decisions. Do not use them in a learner-facing evaluator until each record is completed, tested and signed off.
- APS is generally calculated from the achievement ratings of six school subjects, excluding Life Orientation (Prospectus printed p. 16). This general rule does not prove each programme's full APS subject set, all conditions, or all exceptions.
- Mathematics, Technical Mathematics and Mathematical Literacy are distinct inputs. Do not substitute one for another unless the exact programme rule explicitly permits it.
- A confirmed failure of a verified mandatory rule may produce `NOT_ELIGIBLE`; incomplete, conflicting, unsigned or inapplicable rules must produce `UNKNOWN` unless another independently verified mandatory failure already establishes `NOT_ELIGIBLE`.
- Source statements below are minimums visible in the cited 2027 tables. Additional selection rules, programme-specific notes, and cycle applicability must be checked against the complete applicable source before approval.
