### Cross-cutting source note

The indexed 2027 prospectus states that Technical Mathematics and Technical Science are not accepted for degree programmes in the Faculty of Science. Confirm the exact scope and wording in the full authoritative document before encoding this as a rule. Do not infer that Mathematical Literacy is accepted for these degree programmes. Do not calculate APS from a generic formula: use a verified programme-specific formula and subject inclusion/exclusion rules. Any material missing or conflicting rule must produce `UNKNOWN`.
