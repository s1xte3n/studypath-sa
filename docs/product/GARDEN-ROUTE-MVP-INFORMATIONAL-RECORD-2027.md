# Garden Route District Municipality External Bursary 2027 — MVP disposition

**Decision date:** 2026-10-16  
**Decision owner:** Project owner  
**Decision:** Use the official 2027 municipal notice as the source of an informational MVP funding card. This is not a representation that the municipality has approved StudyPath SA, that the exact 2027 form was verified, or that the learner is eligible or will receive funding.

## Verified record for the MVP

- **Display name:** Garden Route District Municipality External Bursary 2027
- **Provider:** Garden Route District Municipality
- **Cycle:** 2027
- **Official notice:** https://www.gardenroute.gov.za/2026/09/04/04-september-2026-opportunities-financial-assistance-towards-further-studies-2027/
- **Official website:** https://www.gardenroute.gov.za/
- **Deadline text:** 31 October 2026 (date-only; no time or timezone inferred)
- **Application window opened:** 1 September 2026
- **Study scope:** Full-time study at SAQA-accredited tertiary or TVET institutions
- **Applicant scope in notice:** Current Grade 12 learners, Grade 12 completers, and current tertiary students
- **Geographic condition:** Applicant's parents must permanently reside in the Garden Route municipal area
- **Application route:** Follow the official municipal notice and confirm the current application form and submission instructions directly with the municipality.
- **Amount/coverage:** Not stated in this MVP card; do not infer.
- **Eligibility result:** `UNKNOWN` unless and until all applicable, non-sensitive criteria have authoritative evidence. A card impression is not an eligibility determination.

## Display and privacy rules

1. Link directly to the official notice. Do not link an older PDF as though it were confirmed as the 2027 form.
2. Display the deadline exactly as a date-only source fact. Do not invent a closing time, timezone, extension, award amount, or additional criteria.
3. Explain that this is a local opportunity and the parental permanent-residence condition applies.
4. Do not collect or store identity numbers, certified identity documents, household income, parental income affidavits, proof-of-address documents, bank details, uploaded application forms, or any other supporting application documents.
5. StudyPath SA does not submit applications and does not guarantee eligibility or funding.
6. The municipality's notice is verified as the source for the facts above; the exact 2027 application-form version and complete award/selection rules remain unconfirmed.

## Release boundary

This owner decision approves the record for informational MVP inclusion. It does **not** by itself pass the funding API publication gate in `docs/api/API-CONTRACT.md`, which requires 3–5 curator-reviewed opportunities, nor does it prove any runtime exists. The repository currently contains no verified application/API runtime for this record. Implementing the display and verifying the complete student journey, privacy behavior, tests and CI remain required before calling it live.
